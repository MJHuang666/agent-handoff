# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, appearance, disability, nationality, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and technical work rather than people.
- Respect different tool preferences and experience levels.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, reject, or temporarily restrict contributions that violate this code. Reports are handled as privately as practical.
